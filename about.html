Our story

A small restaurant with a big heart, built on the simple idea that good food and good company go together.

What's in a name

Simplicity, the Timplicity way

Timplicity is a little play on a big idea - simplicity. We believe the best meals aren't the most complicated ones; they're the honest ones, made with care and shared without fuss.

From the moment you walk through our doors at the De Palm Lifestyle Centre, the goal is the same: make you feel at home. A friendly face, a comfortable table, a plate of food that tastes like someone cooked it just for you.

We're proud to be part of St Helena Bay — a beautiful corner of the West Coast where the pace is gentle, the people are warm, and there's always time for a proper meal by the bay.
