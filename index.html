Welcome to Timplicity

Where good food and good company meet

Tucked into the De Palm Lifestyle Centre in St Helena Bay, Timplicity was built on a simple idea: real, honest food served with a warm heart.

There's no fuss here — just generous plates, friendly faces and the kind of atmosphere that makes you want to stay a little longer. Whether you're popping in for a relaxed breakfast, a long lunch by the bay, or our much-loved Sunday buffet, you'll always leave full and smiling.

The local favourite

The Sunday Buffet everyone talks about

Bring the whole family and settle in. Our Sunday buffet is a St Helena Bay tradition — a generous spread of comforting favourites, fresh sides and something sweet to finish.
